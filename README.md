# 🎬 Movie Explorer Pro (TMDB Edition)

**Movie Explorer Pro** is a modern, cinema-grade React web application powered by **The Movie Database (TMDB) API**. Explore real-time trending movies, binge-worthy TV series, 4K backdrop art, official YouTube trailers, full cast profiles, and curate your personalized cinema lounge.

---

## 🚀 Key TMDB Features & Highlights

### 🍿 1. Live TMDB Exploration & Discovery
- **🔥 Live Trending Content**: Real-time trending movies & TV shows from TMDB (`/trending/all/day`).
- **🍿 Now Playing In Theaters**: Currently running theatrical blockbusters.
- **🏆 All-Time Top Rated Masterpieces**: The highest-rated films in cinema history.
- **🚀 Upcoming Anticipated Releases**: Next blockbuster releases to plan your watchlist.
- **📺 Binge-Worthy TV Series**: Top television series with season & episode counts.
- **🎭 Instant Genre Discovery**: Quick genre chips (Action, Sci-Fi, Drama, Comedy, Horror, Animation, etc.) powered by TMDB's Discover endpoint.
- **🎬 Cinematic Hero Banner**: Dynamic premiere spotlight with 4K backdrops, synopsis, tagline, and instant trailer launch.

### 🎥 2. Rich Media & Interactive Details
- **▶️ Direct TMDB Official Trailers**: High-definition YouTube trailers and teasers retrieved directly from TMDB's video library.
- **👥 Cast & Crew Showcase**: Full actor cast with photo avatars, character roles, and clickable actor search.
- **✨ "More Like This" Recommendations**: Interactive similar & recommended titles inside every movie modal.
- **💰 Box Office & Budget**: Real production budget and worldwide box office financials.
- **⚡ Live Debounced Search**: Fast autocomplete with movie and TV show thumbnails.

### 🎯 3. Personal Cinema Lounge
- **🔖 Custom Watchlist**: Save movies to your private list stored in `localStorage`.
- **❤️ Favorites Collection**: 1-click favorite toggling.
- **✅ Watched Tracker**: Track what you've watched.
- **⭐ 1–5 Star Ratings & Mini-Reviews**: Rate films and write personal reviews.
- **📊 Cinema Taste Profile & Analytics**: Visual genre breakdown, average personal rating, and library statistics.
- **📦 Backup & Restore**: Export and import your entire collection as JSON.

### ✨ 4. Aesthetic & Design System
- **Cinematic Dark & Light Modes**: Glassmorphic cards, glowing accents, and ambient contrast.
- **Skeleton Shimmer Loaders**: Smooth loading states for zero layout shift.
- **Toast Notifications**: Floating interactive feedback for all library actions.
